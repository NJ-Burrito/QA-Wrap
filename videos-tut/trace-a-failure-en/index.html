More tutorials, inside the tool
A page breaks.
The console shows a red 500.
But which click caused it?
QA Wrap keeps every click,
request and error on one timeline.
When a request fails,
QA Wrap links it to the click right before it.
So the click that broke the page is named,
with its time.
Open the failure in the report,
and the click sits right beside it.
No guessing, and no digging through the console.
Just the step that broke it.
Hand it over,
and a developer can repeat it in seconds.
