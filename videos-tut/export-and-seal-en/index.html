More tutorials, inside the tool
A seal shows a change. It hides nothing.
When the session is done,
open More and choose Timeline, sealed.
It is a plain text file:
every event, in order, readable by anyone.
Your name from Settings is written at the top.
At the end sits a seal: your name,
the date, a fingerprint of the text, and a signature.
Change one word by hand,
and the seal no longer matches.
In Settings, press Verify a sealed file.
QA Wrap says intact or altered,
and who sealed it.
A seal shows a change.
It hides nothing,
and it does not prove who you are.
