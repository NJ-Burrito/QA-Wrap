Appearance and language

Language

Which language QA Wrap writes its own screens in. It does not touch the page you are testing, and it changes here without a reload.

Automatic follows the language your browser itself is set to. Reports and exported files keep the wording they had: this is the interface, not the artefacts.

Theme

Light, night, or follow your machine. The button in the top bar cycles the same choice.

Sound

Short, quiet cues from a CC0 sound pack, played from files that ship with the extension. Results and alerts is eight moments; Every click adds seven quiet touch sounds. Neither plays in the in-page toolbar or while the microphone is on.

On by default, and additional to everything. Nothing is announced only by sound, so switching it off costs nothing. That is the test for whether an audio cue belongs in a tool at all.

Which sounds

Effects are transforms and fades only, under half a second, and never run when your system asks for reduced motion. They cost nothing measurable; the suites check.

Shortcuts

Click a shortcut, then press the keys you want. Every shortcut needs at least one modifier (Ctrl, Alt, Shift), otherwise it would fire while you type.

Global shortcuts

These work even when the page is not focused. Chrome reserves the right to set them, so they are edited in Chrome's own shortcuts page. An extension is not allowed to change them for you.

Sessions

Defaults applied when you start recording.

Recording has no time limit. A session runs until you press stop. Screen recording is bounded only by disk space, which is reported below before you start.

Higher records fewer, more settled values. Lower catches fast typing.

How far back QA Wrap looks for the click that caused a failure. Raise it for slow servers.

Layout stress

What happens when you run the RTL / text-expansion check.

Break a request

Defaults for the "break the next request" tool.

Drag to 0 to keep the rule on until you turn it off.

Timeline

How the DevTools panel behaves on a long session.

Older rows stay in the export; this only caps what is drawn.

Permissions

Everything the recorder needs, switched on from here. Each one is asked for once and remembered by the browser.

Sites QA Wrap may work on

Checking...

QA Wrap starts with access to nothing. Turning the switch on in the popup asks for the site you are on, and that answer is remembered until you take it back here. Putting QA Wrap to sleep does not take it back: sleep stops the work, not the permission.

    What each site may do by itself

    None. You choose these in the popup with "Wake up on this site by itself next time".

      Microphone

      Checking...

      For narrating what you are looking at while you record. Nothing is recorded when you press this: the stream is opened to obtain the permission and stopped again immediately.

      At record time Chrome asks nothing more. Once this is allowed the recorder opens the microphone silently, every time.

      Camera

      Checking...

      For the webcam inset in the corner of a recording. Asked for separately from the microphone, so refusing one does not cost you the other.

      At record time Chrome asks nothing more. The camera light comes on while a clip that uses it is running, and not before.

      Screen recording

      Checking...

      Two things stay with Chrome and cannot be switched off here. Chrome always asks which screen or window to share, every time, and no extension may choose one for you. Recording the current tab needs the toolbar button or the shortcut first, because Chrome only grants that at the moment you invoke the extension on that tab, and drops it again when the page navigates.

      Where files go

      The subfolder inside Downloads, used when no folder is chosen above. QA Wrap makes its own QA Wrap folder inside whatever you name here, so there is no need to type that yourself.

      A filename reads <what it is>-dd-mm-yy-hh-mm, so a screenshot taken at half past two on 5 September is screenshot-05-09-26-14-30.png. Colons and slashes are illegal in filenames on every platform, so hh:mm is written hh-mm and dd/mm/yy as dd-mm-yy.

      Integrations

      Every export is a file or a clipboard copy. Connecting a service is the one exception, and only when you press File or Send.

      Token vault

      Saved tokens are stored encrypted and only the background worker can decrypt them. An optional passphrase keeps them locked until you unlock QA Wrap here.

      This defeats disk and profile scanners and sync leaks, not malware running as you while QA Wrap is unlocked.

      Jira issue types

      Which type a defect and a test case are filed as. Left empty, the report picks whatever this project calls them, read from your site rather than guessed.

      Privacy and data protection

      Nothing leaves this machine unless you press a button that says so. What follows is about what stays on it, because a local file full of other people's records is still a file full of other people's records.

      Replay buffer (last 2 minutes)

      Off by default, and turned on one site at a time from the popup. While it is on, QA Wrap keeps the last 2 minutes of your clicks and typing, the console and the network calls (address, status and time only, no bodies) in the memory of that page. Nothing is written to disk and nothing is sent anywhere until you press Save last 2 minutes. Anything older than 2 minutes is dropped, and closing or reloading the page clears it. Sensitive values are masked before they are kept.

      No site has it on.

        Agent bridge

        Off.

        Redaction

        These two are off by default: a ten-digit national number and a ten-digit order reference are the same shape, and a tester testing order references needs to read them.

        How long sessions are kept

        Finished sessions can now expire. QA Wrap can delete a session, with its events, its screenshots and its recordings, once it is older than the window below. Nothing has been deleted: this profile is set to keep everything until you choose a window, because a retention policy applied to history you already had would not have been your decision.

        The session, its events, its screenshots and its recordings: deleted together, daily. A running session is never touched. Zero keeps everything until you delete it yourself.

            What this answers to

            Masking by hand and redaction by shape are data minimisation (GDPR Art. 5(1)(c)) and protection by design and by default (Art. 25, ISO/IEC 27701 cl. 7.4). Retention is storage limitation (Art. 5(1)(e)). Covering values in captures is masking of test data (ISO/IEC 27002:2022 control 8.11). None of this is a certification. It is the set of controls a certification would ask you to show.

            Safe route

            Two ways back. One puts every setting on this page back to how it shipped and leaves your recorded sessions alone. The other deletes the sessions and keeps the settings. Neither one touches the other, and neither can be undone.
