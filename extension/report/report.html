QA Wrap records what you do in the page, then explains what broke and why.

  1. Click the QA Wrap icon in the toolbar.
  2. Name what you are testing. This becomes the report title.
  3. Press Start recording, then use the page normally.
  4. Press Stop. Your report appears right here.

Nothing is uploaded. Everything stays in this browser.
